**Answer:**

Climate action is necessary because inaction would exacerbate the economic impacts of climate change, leading to increased costs and reduced benefits across various sectors. Additionally, the overwhelming consensus among scientists and substantial empirical evidence demonstrate the consequences of inaction.

However, critics argue that the effectiveness of climate action may be overstated, and alternative strategies could address health impacts more effectively. Furthermore, climate action may not address the complexity of the climate crisis or alternative viewpoints adequately.
